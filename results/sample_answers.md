## PD-MATH-X-007

(i) Using factor theorem show that \( (3x + 2) \) is a factor of \( 3x^{3} + 2x^{2} - 3x - 2 \) . Hence, factorise the expression \( 3x^{3} + 2x^{2} - 3x - 2 \) completely. [3]

**Solution**

Let $P(x)=3 x^{3} + 2 x^{2} - 3 x - 2$. The proposed linear factor is zero at $x=- \frac{2}{3}$.

$$P\left(- \frac{2}{3}\right)=3 \left(- \frac{2}{3}\right)^{3} + 2 \left(- \frac{2}{3}\right)^{2} - 3 \left(- \frac{2}{3}\right) - 2=0$$

By the factor theorem, the proposed factor divides $P(x)$.

Divide the polynomial by the proposed factor.

$$3 x^{3} + 2 x^{2} - 3 x - 2=\left(3 x + 2\right) \left(x^{2} - 1\right)$$

Factor the quotient.

$$x^{2} - 1=\left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right)$$

Combine the factors and check by expansion.

$$3 x^{3} + 2 x^{2} - 3 x - 2=\left(x - 1\right) \left(x + 1\right) \left(3 x + 2\right)$$

The zero remainder confirms the proposed factor; the last line is the complete factorisation.

## PD-MATH-X-021

7. एक समांतर श्रेढ़ी का चौथा पद शून्य है। सिद्ध कीजिए कि इसका 25 वां पद, उसके 11 वें पद का तीन गुना है।

The 4$^{th}$ term of an A.P. is zero. Prove that the 25$^{th}$ term of the A.P. is three times its 11$^{th}$ term.

**Solution**

Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference be $d$. Then $T_k=a+(k-1)d$.

Use the given zero term to find the first term in terms of the common difference.

$$a + 3 d = 0\implies a = - 3 d$$

Substitute the first-term relation into the target term.

$$a + 24 d=21 d$$

Substitute the same relation into the comparison term.

$$3 a + 30 d=21 d$$

Thus $T_{25}=21 d=3T_{11}$.

## PD-MATH-X-022

20. तीन समांतर श्रेणियों के प्रथम $n$ पदों का योग क्रमशः $S_1, S_2$ तथा $S_3$ हैं। तीनों का प्रथम पद 1 है तथा सार्व अन्तर क्रमशः 1, 2 तथा 3 हैं। सिद्ध कीजिए कि $S_1 + S_3 = 2S_2$ .

The sums of first $n$ terms of three arithmetic progressions are $S_1, S_2$ and $S_3$ respectively. The first term of each A.P. is 1 and their common differences are 1, 2 and 3 respectively. Prove that $S_1 + S_3 = 2S_2$ .

**Solution**

For an AP with first term $a$ and common difference $d$, $S_n=\frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]$.

For progression 1, $S_1=\frac{n \left(n + 1\right)}{2}$.

For progression 2, $S_2=n^{2}$.

For progression 3, $S_3=\frac{n \left(3 n - 1\right)}{2}$.

Substitute and collect the terms:

$$S_{1} - 2 S_{2} + S_{3}=\frac{n \left(1 + n\right)}{2} - 2 n^{2} + \frac{n \left(-1 + 3 n\right)}{2}=0$$

Hence the sum relation holds for every positive integer $n$.

## PD-MATH-X-030

9. पूर्ण वर्ग बनाने की विधि का प्रयोग करते हुए दर्शाइए कि समीकरण $$x^2 - 8x + 18 = 0$$ का कोई हल नहीं है।

Using completing the square method, show that the equation $$x^2 - 8x + 18 = 0$$ has no solution.

**Solution**

Complete the square:

$$x^{2} - 8 x + 18=\left(x - 4\right)^{2} + 2$$

Since a square of a real number is nonnegative, this expression is strictly positive.

Therefore the equation has no real root.
